## Full Description (500+ chars)

### What is CATCH?

CATCH (캐치 — 퇴직금 한번에) is a free labor rights calculator built specifically for daily and short-term workers in South Korea, especially those working at major logistics centers like Coupang, Kurly, and CJ Logistics.

Millions of daily workers in Korea are legally entitled to severance pay, unemployment benefits, weekly holiday pay, and annual leave compensation — but most never claim them because the calculations are complex and confusing.

CATCH changes that.

### What CATCH calculates:

1. **Severance Pay** — Uses the "28-day reverse block" algorithm required by Korean labor law to determine eligibility and exact payout. Workers need 365+ days total and 8+ workdays per 28-day block.

2. **Unemployment Benefits (구직급여)** — Checks if you've worked 180+ insured days in the past 18 months, then calculates your daily benefit amount and total duration.

3. **Weekly Holiday Pay (주휴수당)** — Calculates the weekly rest day allowance for workers who clock 15+ hours per week.

4. **Annual Leave Pay (연차수당)** — Computes unused annual leave payout based on actual workdays.

### Two Ways to Calculate:

- **PDF Upload (Precise)**: Upload your employment insurance work history PDF (free from Korea's Gov24 portal). CATCH parses it automatically, runs the full algorithm, and shows exact results.
- **Manual Input (Quick)**: Enter start date, end date, and daily wage. Get an instant estimate in seconds.

### Who built this and why?

I'm Jonghun, a former HR recruiter at Coupang Fulfillment Services. I watched thousands of daily workers leave severance pay and unemployment benefits on the table — not because they weren't entitled, but because they didn't know or couldn't figure out the math.

I built CATCH with Claude (Anthropic) to give every daily worker the power to know their rights in under 60 seconds. No signup required. Completely free.

## Hunter's First Comment (Personal Story)

Hi Product Hunt! 👋 I'm Jonghun, and I built CATCH because I saw a problem up close that most people never notice.

For 3+ years, I worked in HR recruitment at Coupang Fulfillment Services — one of Korea's largest logistics operations. Every day, I saw thousands of short-term workers cycling through our facilities: they came in, worked hard, and left. Most never asked about severance pay or unemployment benefits.

At first I assumed they were getting everything they were owed. Then I started asking around. Turns out, most workers had no idea they were even eligible — or the process felt too complicated to bother with.

The severance calculation alone (the "28-day reverse block" method) is genuinely confusing: you have to count backward from your last workday in 28-day chunks, check if each chunk has 8+ days worked, and only count qualifying blocks toward the 365-day threshold. Most workers just... give up.

So I decided to build something. With zero coding experience, I partnered with Claude to build CATCH — a free, mobile-first calculator that does all the hard math automatically. You either upload your employment PDF or enter a few dates manually, and within 60 seconds you know exactly what you're owed.

**CATCH currently covers:**
- Severance pay (퇴직금)
- Unemployment benefits (실업급여)
- Weekly holiday pay (주휴수당)
- Annual leave pay (연차수당)

It's completely free, no login required for calculations, and built specifically for the 5+ million daily workers in Korea who deserve to know their rights.

If you've ever worked a short-term job and wondered "wait, am I owed anything?" — CATCH is for you. Or share it with someone who needs it. 🙏

Would love to hear your feedback in the comments!

**Live app**: https://catch-daily-worker.vercel.app
